Questions
Quick answers about the platform.


Which AI model powers the agents?
Agents run on Groq-hosted open models through CrewAI, so responses are fast.
How are runs monitored?
Every agent call is traced in LangSmith with latency, tokens and errors.
What do I get at the end?
Final Python code, test cases, a review summary and a development report.
Can I choose the model?
Yes. Pick the model and temperature in the Workspace settings.
